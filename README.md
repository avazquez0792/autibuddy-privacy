# Privacy Policy for AutiBuddy

**Last Updated: April 24, 2026**

## Overview

AutiBuddy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families who use our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use AutiBuddy.

## Information We Do Not Collect

AutiBuddy is designed with privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, address, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Photos, camera, or microphone data
- Contacts or social data
- Any data from children under 13

## Information Stored on Your Device

AutiBuddy stores the following information **locally on your device only**:

- Game progress and scores
- Badges and achievements earned
- Daily streak count
- Age mode preference (Toddler, Kids, or Teen)
- Subscription status

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

## Subscription & Purchases

AutiBuddy offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. All billing is handled by Apple in accordance with their privacy policy at [apple.com/privacy](https://www.apple.com/privacy).

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AutiBuddy is designed for use by children under the supervision of a parent or guardian. We take children's privacy very seriously and comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

- We do not collect any personal information from children
- We do not show advertisements
- We do not include links to external websites
- We do not allow in-app messaging or social features
- We do not share any data with third parties

## Third-Party Services

AutiBuddy does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Security

Since AutiBuddy does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of your data being intercepted or compromised through our app. All game data remains stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's built-in security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
